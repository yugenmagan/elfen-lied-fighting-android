# Credits and rights

## Original fan-made PC game

The supplied game's own credit image, `app/src/main/assets/game/0222/0000.png`, identifies the creator as **ねくぱっち** (`製作 ねくぱっち`). This is the confirmed attribution retained by this port. The original credit image and Japanese credits are preserved.

The historical address printed in the game is `http://www.ob2.aitai.ne.jp/~touhou/`. It is included as historical provenance, not as a claim about current ownership or availability.

Guest-character credits in the original material include **ミサキ — ナナパパさん / anesueizi factory** and **ロストナンバー — 背徳三昧 / ねくぱっち**. The historical anesueizi address is `http://www.geocities.jp/anesueizi/index.html`.

## Elfen Lied

**Elfen Lied / エルフェンリート** is the work of **Lynn Okamoto / 岡本倫**; the manga is published by **Shueisha / 集英社**. Publisher reference: [Shueisha's official book entry](https://www.shueisha.co.jp/books/items/contents.html?jdcn=08876358876358315501).

This fan port is unofficial and is not endorsed by them. Attribution does not grant the port maintainer ownership of those materials or a new license to them.

## Android port

The Android runtime, input, lifecycle handling, conversion/recovery work, localization overlays and build tooling are port work. The port maintainer does not claim authorship of the original game or its characters and assets.

- Launcher icon source: original Lucy portrait `game/0170/0135.png`.
- Title screen source: `デモ 2.demo`, extracted pack `game/0074`, image `0011.png`; referenced by the original KGT screen table.
- Combat HUD, portraits, selection artwork, sprites, backgrounds, sounds and music: supplied original game data; provenance and extraction details are recorded in `PORTING_NOTES.md` and `FORMAT_NOTES.md`.

The project uses the Android platform APIs. Gradle and Android build tools retain their respective upstream licenses. No blanket open-source license is asserted over the original game assets by this repository.
